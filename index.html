WhisperKeyboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

WhisperKeyboard ("the app") is a voice-dictation app with a companion keyboard for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: speech recognition runs on your device, we do not collect your data, and nothing is used for tracking or advertising.

Audio and speech recognition

  • The app records audio only between your start and stop taps, to turn your speech into text.
  • Speech is transcribed entirely on your device (NVIDIA Parakeet, OpenAI Whisper or Apple's Speech framework). Audio is never sent to us or any server and is not stored after transcription.
  • Speech models are downloaded once from Hugging Face on first use. This download contains no personal information.

The keyboard

  • The keyboard does not record, store or transmit what you type.
  • "Full Access" is used only to receive dictated text from the WhisperKeyboard app through a shared container on your device.
  • iOS never uses third-party keyboards in password fields.

Optional AI rewrite

  • By default, text rewriting uses Apple Intelligence on your device, or is switched off.
  • If you choose to connect Claude on Amazon Bedrock with your own (or your organisation's) Bedrock API key, the text you ask to rewrite is sent directly from your device to Amazon Bedrock. Audio is never sent. The app asks for your consent before sending any text, and you can withdraw it in Settings at any time.
  • That processing is governed by your or your organisation's agreement with Amazon Web Services. We do not receive or store this text.
  • Your Bedrock API key is stored only in your device's Keychain.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, custom modes and dictation history are stored only on your device. You can delete history in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes all of it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • We do not create accounts; no login is required.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
